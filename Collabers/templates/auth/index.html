ABOUT


Collabers is a smart web platform designed to seamlessly connect brands with the right influencers for marketing collaborations. It simplifies the discovery, application, and selection process using an intuitive UI and data-driven filtering. Built with modern technology and machine learning, Collabers makes influencer marketing smarter and faster.

FEATURES

AI-Powered Influencer Sorting

Uses an XGBoost machine learning model to rank influencers based on engagement rate, follower count, and niche relevance.

Helps brands quickly identify high-potential influencers without manual screening.

Makes the selection process data-driven and efficient, reducing guesswork and bias.

Role-Based Dashboards

Provides a personalized experience based on whether the user is a brand or an influencer.

Influencers see campaign listings, application statuses, and profile metrics.

Brands get tools to create, track, and manage campaigns, along with influencer applications.

Campaign Discovery & Management

Influencers can search and filter campaigns based on category, budget, and deadline.

Brands can create and publish campaigns with detailed requirements and goals.

Both roles can track applications, view status updates, and manage ongoing collaborations from their dashboard.

HOW ITS WORKS

Our 5-Step Smart Matching Process :

Sign Up & Choose Your Role :

Users register as either an Influencer or a Brand. Each role gets a unique dashboard tailored to their needs.

Brands Post Campaigns :

Brands create collaboration opportunities by setting campaign details like category, deliverables, budget, and deadline.

Influencers Discover & Apply :

Influencers browse available campaigns, filter by niche, and apply by submitting a pitch with their profile data.

Smart Application Review :

The system uses a machine learning model (XGBoost) to rank influencer applications based on engagement rate, relevance, and other metrics.

Brands Shortlist & Collaborate :

Brands review ranked applications and shortlist the best candidates to proceed with collaboration outside or within the platform.
